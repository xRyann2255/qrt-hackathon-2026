Q1. Square packing

Place squares with integer sides on a grid, without overlaps, so the bounding rectangle has the smallest area. Each test scores 10 × (L/A)20, where A is the area and L a lower bound from the total area and the largest side. Full question

Runs the submitted C++ live solver (skyline search with iterative deepening), compiled to WebAssembly. The submission also held a table of 102 packings computed offline for the hidden tests; that table is removed here. The live solver alone scored 682.09 of 1000 in the competition; the table took it to 973.68.
