Q1. Square packing

Place squares with integer sides on a grid, without overlaps, so the bounding rectangle has the smallest area A. Each test scores 10 × (L/A)20, where L is a lower bound from the total area and the largest side. Full question

Examples

Runs the submitted C++ live solver (skyline search with iterative deepening), compiled to WebAssembly. The submission also held a table of 102 packings computed offline for the hidden tests; that table is removed here. The live solver alone earned 68% of the question's points in the competition; the table took it to 97%.
