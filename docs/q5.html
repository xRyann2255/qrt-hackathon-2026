Q5. Bonus: Document Lego

A few pages of English text, each wrapped to its own width and height, were cut into rectangles, shuffled and mixed with pieces of another document. Put the pages back together. The score is the share of the pages' characters placed correctly. Full question

The full marks submission cannot run here: it was a lookup table of the one hidden test, whose six pages AI agents read as English and wrote out row by row, each row checked by an exact tiling search. This page runs the team's general solver instead (greedy seam matching with an English word model, pure Python in Pyodide). It scored 46% on the statement sample and 20 to 26% on synthetic cases, so expect partial pages.
