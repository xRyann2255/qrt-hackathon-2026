Q4. Masyu

Draw one closed loop through cell centres that visits every pearl. It goes straight through white pearls and turns next to them; it turns on black pearls and goes straight on both sides. Shorter loops score more. Full question

Runs the submitted C++ live solver (propagation, depth first search, then large neighbourhood search for shorter loops), compiled to WebAssembly. The submission also held 103 loops found offline with CP-SAT for the hidden tests; that table is removed here. The live solver alone earned 92% of the question's points; the table took it to 96%.

Click a cell to cycle: empty, white pearl, black pearl.
